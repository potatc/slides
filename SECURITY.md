## Security & Contact Policy

We take project security and user privacy seriously. Please note our official communication policy:

1. **Official Email**: `contact@slides.potatc.org`
2. **No Proactive Outreach**: 
   We will **NEVER** proactively send you unprompted emails. We only reply after receiving an inquiry from you. Our response will always include the original content/quoted text of your initial email as context. Any spontaneous email claiming to be from us is a phishing attempt.
3. **No Attachments/Payments**: 
   We will never ask for your passwords, send suspicious `.exe`/`.apk` attachments, or request payments via email.
